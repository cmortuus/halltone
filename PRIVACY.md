# Halltone Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

Halltone streams audio from your Mac to your iPhone over your own local network. It is built to
work without collecting anything about you.

**What Halltone collects: nothing.** Neither the iPhone app nor the Mac app sends any data to the
developer or to any third party. There are no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or
crash-reporting services.

**What stays on your devices**

- **Audio** travels directly from your Mac to your iPhone over your local network. It never leaves
  your network and is not recorded or stored.
- **Pairing codes** you enter on the iPhone are stored in the iPhone's Keychain so you don't have
  to re-enter them.
- **Settings** (such as the Macs you added by IP address and the auto-connect switch) are stored
  on the device.
- **A diagnostics log** (connection state, audio route changes, buffer statistics) is kept on the
  iPhone, capped at about 2 MB. It contains no audio and no personal information beyond your
  device and Mac names. It is only shared if you choose "Share diagnostics log" and send it
  somewhere yourself.

**Permissions**

- **Local Network** (iPhone and Mac): to find your Mac and receive audio from it.
- **System Audio Recording** (Mac): to capture what your Mac is playing so it can be streamed.
  Captured audio is sent only to iPhones on your network that you have paired with the Mac's
  pairing code.
- **Accessibility** (Mac, optional): only if you use the lock-screen play/pause/skip controls,
  so the Mac app can press the media keys for you.

**Children.** Halltone does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this address.
