# Contributing to Halltone

Thanks for helping. Bug reports, fixes, and ideas are all welcome; please open an issue first for
anything large.

## Licensing of contributions

Halltone is licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later (see `LICENSE`).

The official iPhone app is also distributed through Apple's App Store, whose terms are generally
considered incompatible with the GPL for code the publisher doesn't own. So that the official
build can include your work, by submitting a contribution you agree that:

1. your contribution is licensed to everyone under GPL-3.0-or-later, **and**
2. you additionally grant the Halltone maintainers permission to distribute your contribution as
   part of Halltone through the Apple App Store and Mac App Store under those stores' terms.

You keep the copyright to your contribution. If you don't agree to point 2, say so in your pull
request and we'll discuss it.
